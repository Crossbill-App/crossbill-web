## What it does

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Web ebook reader" icon="laptop">
		Upload an EPUB and read it in the browser. The reader opens where you left
		off on any device. Jump from a highlight to its place in the book, and
		make new highlights.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Sync from KOReader" icon="cloud-download">
		Optional. The KOReader plugin uploads your highlights, highlight styles and
		reading sessions. Highlights you make in the web reader sync back to the
		device.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Organize" icon="list-format">
		Group highlights and notes with your own tags, cluster those tags into tag
		groups, and name the labels your e-reader's highlight styles stand for.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Write notes" icon="pencil">
		Write notes about a term, a character, a concept or the gist of a chapter.
		Link them to the book's chapters, highlights and tags.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Chapter digests" icon="open-book">
		Generate a summary, key points and comprehension questions for a chapter.
		Read them before the chapter to skim it, or after it for review. Works with
		Ollama, OpenAI, Anthropic and Gemini.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Flashcards" icon="random">
		Make question-and-answer cards from highlights, chapters and notes, or let
		the AI suggest them. Study them in Anki.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Search by meaning" icon="magnifier">
		Find highlights, notes and chapters by meaning, across all books and
		languages. Optional. Needs an embedding provider.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Book reflections" icon="approve-check-circle">
		Answer Adler's four analytical-reading questions about a book. Track how
		far you are with each book with a reading stage.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

Crossbill is **self-hosted and multi-user**. Your data stays on your own
server.
