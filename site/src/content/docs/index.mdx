## What it does

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Sync from KOReader" icon="cloud-download">
		The KOReader plugin uploads your highlights, their highlight styles and your
		reading sessions. Re-syncing the same book does not duplicate anything.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Organize" icon="list-format">
		Group highlights and notes with your own tags, cluster those tags into tag
		groups, and name the labels your e-reader's highlight styles stand for.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Write notes" icon="pencil">
		Author notes about a term, a character, a concept or the gist of a chapter,
		and link them to the book's chapters, highlights and tags.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Chapter digests" icon="open-book">
		Generate a summary, key points and comprehension questions for a chapter —
		read them before the chapter to skim, or after it as review. Ollama, OpenAI,
		Anthropic and Gemini are supported.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Flashcards" icon="random">
		Turn highlights, chapters and notes into question-and-answer cards, let the
		AI suggest them for you, and study them in Anki.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Search by meaning" icon="magnifier">
		Find a highlight, note or chapter by what it was about rather than by the
		words in it — across every book, and across languages. Optional: it needs an
		embedding provider.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Book reflections" icon="approve-check-circle">
		Answer Adler's four analytical-reading questions about a book, and track
		where each book stands with a reading stage.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

Crossbill is **self-hosted and multi-user** — every book, highlight and note
belongs to one account on your own server, and your reading stays there.
