[Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/) is a spaced-repetition program. The Crossbill
add-on syncs your [flashcards](../../features/flashcards/) and highlights from
Crossbill into your Anki collection. You then study the cards in Anki.

## Installing

The installation and configuration steps are in the add-on repository:

- [Crossbill-App/anki-addon](https://github.com/Crossbill-App/anki-addon)

You need your Crossbill server address and an account on it.
