[Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/) is a spaced-repetition program. Crossbill is a
good place to make cards out of what you read; Anki is a good place to actually
drill them. The Crossbill add-on connects the two.

## What it does

The add-on integrates your Crossbill highlights and the
[flashcards](../../features/flashcards/) you built from them into your Anki
collection, so the cards you accepted in Crossbill turn up in Anki for review
and Anki's scheduler takes it from there.

## Getting it

Installation and configuration instructions are kept with the add-on:

- [Crossbill-App/anki-addon](https://github.com/Crossbill-App/anki-addon)

You will need your Crossbill server address and an account on it.
