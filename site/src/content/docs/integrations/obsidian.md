title: Obsidian
description: Bring your Crossbill highlights into an Obsidian vault.
---

If your writing already lives in [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/), the Crossbill
Obsidian plugin brings your highlights across so you can quote and build on them
where you take the rest of your notes.

## What it does

The plugin connects your vault to your Crossbill server and integrates your
highlights into your Obsidian notes. Crossbill stays the place your highlights
are synced to and organized; Obsidian becomes another place you can use them
from.

## Getting it

Installation and configuration instructions are kept with the plugin:

- [Crossbill-App/obsidian-plugin](https://github.com/Crossbill-App/obsidian-plugin)

You will need your Crossbill server address and an account on it.
