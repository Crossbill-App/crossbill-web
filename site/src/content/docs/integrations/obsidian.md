---
title: Obsidian
description: Bring your Crossbill highlights into an Obsidian vault.
---

The Crossbill [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) plugin connects your vault to
your Crossbill server and brings your highlights into your Obsidian notes. You
can then quote and link them next to your other notes.

## Installing

The installation and configuration steps are in the plugin repository:

- [Crossbill-App/obsidian-plugin](https://github.com/Crossbill-App/obsidian-plugin)

You need your Crossbill server address and an account on it.
